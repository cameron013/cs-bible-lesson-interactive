# Interactive Christian Science Bible Lesson

A reusable Grok Skill that turns *this week's* Christian Science Bible Lesson into a structured, personalized study.

The Skill does not replace the Bible, *Science and Health*, or the Christian Science Quarterly. It helps you apply the Lesson you already have to the life you are actually in.

## What this closes

Most of us can read the Lesson. The gap is application: this week's citations speaking to this week's work, loves, and challenges, without adding a second textbook.

The Skill forces three things before it teaches:

1. Confirm the exact Lesson in front of you.
2. Confirm your season in your own words.
3. Stay inside this week's citations when it applies the text.

## What you need

- Your own copy of this week's Lesson (MyBibleLesson, the Quarterly, or a PDF you are licensed to use)
- A Grok account with Skills (grok.com, then Automations, then Skills)
- This `SKILL.md` file

Do not upload or republish the official Lesson file as part of this repo. The Lesson remains CSPS material. This repo is only the method.

## Install in Grok (about two minutes)

1. Open [grok.com/automations](https://grok.com/automations) and choose **Skills**.
2. Click **New Skill**.
3. Name it `cs-bible-lesson-interactive`.
4. Paste the full contents of `SKILL.md`.
5. Save.
6. Click **Try it now**.
7. Upload your Lesson PDF, or paste the citations you want to study.
8. When Grok asks for your season, fill the three placeholders. See `SEASON.md`.

Same file works as a system prompt in other models if you paste it at the top of a new chat and then attach the Lesson.

On Gemini, paste it into a **Gem** (moving to **Skills** later in 2026). On Claude, save it as a Skill.

## The three placeholders

Replace the brackets. Keep the lines short.

```
Professional state and direction: [current work or search] moving toward [where you want to go]
What you love and what matters most: [people, practice, church work, craft, health]
Challenges on the table this week: [the specific pressure, claim, or decision]
```

That is the only personalization the Skill is allowed to use.

## What a run looks like

0. Grok names the week and Subject and waits for "yes."
1. Grok restates your three lines and waits for corrections.
2. Golden Text and Responsive Reading, plus a short paraphrase aimed at your season.
3. Each Lesson section: Theme, Bible takeaways, Mary Baker Eddy from *that section only*.
4. A short close with one practice for the next day.
5. Optional panel. You name the voices. They may only speak from this week's citations.

On camera, run steps 0 to 3 for one section, then jump to a two-voice panel. A full six-section study is for later, not for a first demo.

## What this is not

- Not a substitute for the books or class instruction
- Not a public dump of the Quarterly
- Not a biography file. No stored age, title, compensation, or family detail lives in the Skill
- Not extra metaphysics. If it is not in this week's citations, it does not belong in the answer
